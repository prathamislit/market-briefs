### Personal Income and Outlays, October 2026

- **Published:** 2026-11-25T13:30:00+00:00
- **Access:** extractive_excerpt
- **Detail:** Personal income increased $201.8 billion (0.7 percent at a monthly rate) in October, according to estimates released today by the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA). Disposable personal income (DPI)–personal income less personal current taxes–increased $200.5 billion (0.8 percent), and personal consumption expenditures (PCE) increased $63.8 billion (0.3 percent). Personal outlays–the sum of PCE, personal interest payments, and personal current transfer payments–increased $69.3 billion in October. Personal saving was $1.00 trillion, and the personal saving rate–personal saving as a percentage of DPI–was 4.1 percent.
